The rule

The first dial books the call. It does not pitch.

A person who sends a form gets one thing on the first dial: a thirty-minute consultation at a time they pick. No price, no procedure, no records ask, unless they have twenty minutes right now. Voicemail gets the same offer in twenty seconds. Follow-up dials confirm the email landed and book; they do not re-pitch.

Script 1: first-contact dial to a form lead (live answer) · three minutes

1"Hi, this is [name] from Williams Cancer Institute. Am I speaking with [first name]?" [wait] "You filled out our form on [day] asking about treatment. This call is recorded for quality; is that okay?" [wait]

If no: "I've stopped the recording." Continue.

Wrong person / never filled out a form: apologize, remove the number, end.

2"I'm sorry you're dealing with this. I'm not going to run through everything on a two-minute call; you deserve a proper conversation. I'd like to book thirty minutes with me or a colleague at a time that works for you. Do you have twenty minutes right now, or should we pick a time?"

Twenty minutes now: go to Script 6, from "Am I speaking with the patient, or a family member?"

Pick a time: continue.

3"What day works, and is morning or afternoon better? … [day/time]. Who should be on that call with you?"

4"I'm sending a calendar invite and a short email now with my direct line. Have the most recent scan report and pathology report handy for the call; you don't need to send anything yet."

5"Three things so the call is worth your time: all treatment is in Mexico; we don't accept insurance, families pay directly and we'll talk through how; and treatment starts at $35,000 per session, plan on a minimum of one to two sessions, your doctor makes that decision from your file and you'll know before you commit to anything. I'll pause there."

Cost is a hard stop: "Understood. The review stays free if that changes. I won't take more of your time today." End; no booking after a cost-no.

"Is that per session?": "Yes, per session. Plan on a minimum of one to two; your doctor decides from your file, and you'll know before you commit."

Any other question: "Let's start there on [day]; I've written it down."

6"So: [day/time], you and [who], I call you at [number]. Talk then."

Never on this dial: the procedure beyond "the physician injects immunotherapy into the tumor under imaging," records, outcomes, FDA, financing detail, urgency.

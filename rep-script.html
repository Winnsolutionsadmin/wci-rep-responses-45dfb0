The rep script (v4, outbound-first)

Draft for ratification. Six scripts by dial type; the fact sheet is the only source of facts. Amber lines are the forks.

2026-09-05. Rebuilt after the direction check: 86% of the analyzed calls are dials the reps placed to people who submitted a form. Jarred's rule: inbound leads schedule the call themselves rather than receiving a random dial. Scripts 1–3 below are rep-initiated unscheduled dials and are SUPERSEDED pending his answer on leads who never self-book; Script 6 (the scheduled consultation) is the primary script. Price line as ratified 2026-09-05. Insurance, FDA and one-pager lines carry the open notes from counsel deliberation #3 and are unchanged pending his call. Nothing here is applied.

The rule

Jarred, 2026-09-05: inbound leads schedule a call; they do not get a random call. The form, and the automatic reply that follows it, carry the booking calendar; the lead picks the time; the rep's first contact is the consultation at that time. Reps do not place unscheduled dials to leads. What happens with a lead who never self-books is an open decision (below), not a script.

Fact sheet (ratified lines; every script reads from it)

  • Price: "Treatment starts at $35,000 per session. Plan on a minimum of one to two sessions; your doctor makes that decision based on his review of your file, and you'll know before you commit to anything."
  • Location: "Our administrative office is in California. All treatment is in Mexico, Los Cabos or Mexico City."
  • Insurance (v3 wording, panel note open): "We don't accept insurance and can't bill it. Families pay directly, and it's a no on insurance, not a no on getting here. Most families use some mix of savings, an unsecured personal loan from a US lender (several of them lend up to $100,000 and pay the money to you, not to us), family help, and sometimes a fundraiser. I'll email a one-page list of the options with the invite. We're not lenders or advisors, so the list points you to the lenders and the government resources, and if you want someone to walk through it with you, tell me and I'll set that up."
  • Review: "The case review is free. It needs your most recent scans and pathology. The medical team reviews it; then you speak with the physician."
  • FDA (only when asked; panel note open): "FDA status depends on the specific drugs, devices and uses the physician would propose. Treatment is offered only in Mexico. The physician identifies each component's status after review."
  • Outcomes, safety, timeframes: reps never state them.

Script 1: first-contact dial to a form lead (live answer) · three minutes

1"Hi, this is [name] from Williams Cancer Institute. Am I speaking with [first name]?" [wait] "You filled out our form on [day] asking about treatment. This call is recorded for quality; is that okay?" [wait]

If no: "I've stopped the recording." Continue.

Wrong person / never filled out a form: apologize, remove the number, end.

2"I'm sorry you're dealing with this. I'm not going to run through everything on a two-minute call; you deserve a proper conversation. I'd like to book thirty minutes with me or a colleague at a time that works for you. Do you have twenty minutes right now, or should we pick a time?"

Twenty minutes now: go to Script 6, from "Am I speaking with the patient, or a family member?"

Pick a time: continue.

3"What day works, and is morning or afternoon better? … [day/time]. Who should be on that call with you?"

4"I'm sending a calendar invite and a short email now with my direct line. Have the most recent scan report and pathology report handy for the call; you don't need to send anything yet."

5"Three things so the call is worth your time: all treatment is in Mexico; we don't accept insurance, families pay directly and we'll talk through how; and treatment starts at $35,000 per session, plan on a minimum of one to two sessions, your doctor makes that decision from your file and you'll know before you commit to anything. I'll pause there."

Cost is a hard stop: "Understood. The review stays free if that changes. I won't take more of your time today." End; no booking after a cost-no.

"Is that per session?": "Yes, per session. Plan on a minimum of one to two; your doctor decides from your file, and you'll know before you commit."

Any other question: "Let's start there on [day]; I've written it down."

6"So: [day/time], you and [who], I call you at [number]. Talk then."

Never on this dial: the procedure beyond "the physician injects immunotherapy into the tumor under imaging," records, outcomes, FDA, financing detail, urgency.

Script 2: first-contact dial reaches voicemail · twenty seconds

"Hi [first name], this is [name] from Williams Cancer Institute, returning the form you sent on [day]. I'd like to set up a thirty-minute call at a time that suits you. I'm emailing you a link to pick a time, plus my direct line, [number]. Nothing to prepare; I'll answer your questions on that call." No price, no procedure, no Mexico on a machine. Log the dial; the next contact is the email and one follow-up dial (Script 3) in two business days.

Script 3: follow-up dial (email sent, no booking yet) · two minutes

1"Hi [first name], [name] from Williams Cancer Institute; we sent you an email on [day] with a link to book a call, and I wanted to make sure it reached you. Is now a bad time?"

2"I'm not calling to go through everything on the phone. The point of the booked call is that you have the time and the right person with you. Do you want to pick a time now, or do you want me to stop calling and leave the link with you?"

Picks a time: Script 1, steps 3–6.

"Stop calling": "Done. The link stays live and the review stays free. My direct line is in the email." Mark do-not-dial; no third dial.

"I just wanted general information": "The email has our locations, the price line and what the review needs. If you want a conversation later, the link is in it." End.

Records already requested (the chase case): "The upload link is in the email from [day]; if the records are with the hospital, I'll resend the release for them. Do you want me to stay on the line while you find it?" Then Script 6 step 5.

Script 4: dial after an earlier inbound call · two minutes

"Hi [first name], [name] from Williams Cancer Institute. You called us on [day] and [reached voicemail / spoke with a colleague]. I'm calling to set up the proper conversation you asked for; thirty minutes at a time you pick. Twenty minutes now, or a time?" Then Script 1 steps 3–6 or Script 6.

Script 5: inbound, live (14% of calls)

"Thanks for calling Williams Cancer Institute, this is [name]. This line can be recorded for quality; is that okay?" [wait] "I'm sorry you're dealing with this. If you have twenty minutes, I can walk through it properly now; if not, let's book thirty minutes at a time that suits you." Twenty minutes: Script 6. Otherwise Script 1 steps 3–6.

Inbound voicemail: the email with the booking link plus Script 4 next business day.

Script 6: the consultation call · thirty minutes · goal: authorization signed and upload started, or a clean no

1"This is [name] from Williams Cancer Institute for our [time] call; is now still good? This call can be recorded for quality, okay?" (If no: "I've stopped the recording." Continue.)

2"Am I speaking with the patient, or a family member? … When we booked, you wanted to make sure we covered [logged question]. Let's start there."

3"Tell me where things stand. What's the diagnosis, what have you done so far, and what did the doctor say after the last scan?" Reflect once: "So this is [type], [treatments so far], and the last scans in [month] still showed the tumor. Did I get that right?"

Caller states clear remission: "The team only reviews cases with an active tumor on recent imaging. If that changes, call me." End warmly.

4"The physician injects immunotherapy drugs directly into the tumor under image guidance, sometimes with cryoablation. Which drugs, and whether ablation is added, is the physician's call after reviewing the scans. I won't go further than that; I'm not the doctor."

5"Before I ask you to send anything: treatment starts at $35,000 per session. Plan on a minimum of one to two sessions; your doctor makes that decision based on his review of your file, and you'll know before you commit to anything. You'll have it in writing. We don't take insurance, and here's how families handle that." [insurance line from the fact sheet] "I'll stop there. What's your reaction?"

Cost is a hard stop: "Understood. I won't take records if cost is the wall. The review stays free if anything changes." End. No discount, no urgency.

"Does it work?": first, "I don't quote success rates; it depends on the type, the stage and the scans, and I'd be guessing. That's the physician's conversation after review." Second, "I understand why you're asking. I still won't put a number on it." Third, "I'm going to stop there; anything else would be me making it up. Let's get the records in and put that to the doctor."

6"If you want the team to look at the case, the review is free. It needs the most recent scans and pathology you have. I'm emailing the authorization and a secure upload link right now; I'll stay on while it lands. If the records are with the hospital, I'll send the release they need and you hand it to them; we can't pull records ourselves." The patient signs the authorization.

7"Once the packet is complete I call you when the team has looked. We're aiming for two business days, and I'll tell you if it's slipping. Let's put the packet check on the calendar now: [day/time]."

8"So: I've sent the authorization and the link, you send scans and pathology, I call you [day]. Did I get that right? If you decide this isn't for you, tell me; that's fine."

Empathy: three beats only (opening sentence, the reflection, the pause after money). The physician consultation is booked only after the review; the rep never previews outcomes.

Never say

success rate, percentage, "most patients" · guarantee, cure, remission prediction · "no side effects," "minimally invasive" as a safety answer · any pathology statistic · "FDA approved / cleared / registered" · stop, delay or replace current treatment · interpreting scans or pathology · "you qualify / you're a candidate" · "we treat all types / all stages" · any price below $35,000 or a course figure the doctor hasn't set · "insurance might cover / reimburse" · "financing" or "payment plan" as a WCI product · "reverse mortgage," "home equity," "borrow against" as a suggestion · a specific APR or monthly payment · "the consultation is virtual" as a promise · stay length or duration before ratification · pulling hospital records ourselves · drug brand names · urgency or scarcity · "I understand how you feel" · the price band or the procedure on a first dial or a voicemail.

Test before rollout

Randomize by lead within rep, 8 weeks or sequential. Co-primaries: consultation calls held per 100 form leads; written estimates issued per 100 leads. Gate: complaint, refund-request and attorney-contact rate non-inferior; first-dial pitches = 0; price-surprise callbacks; third-press outcome breaks; unscheduled re-dials after "stop calling" = 0. Spanish first-contact volume (13%) needs a Spanish Script 1 and 2 before the test covers those reps.

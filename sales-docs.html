Sales docs: fact sheet, FAQ, never-say, options list, financing

Everything a rep reads from. Price line ratified 2026-09-05; insurance, FDA and the one-pager carry open notes from counsel deliberation #3.

Fact sheet

  • Price: "Treatment starts at $35,000 per session. Plan on a minimum of one to two sessions; your doctor makes that decision based on his review of your file, and you'll know before you commit to anything."
  • Location: "Our administrative office is in California. All treatment is in Mexico, Los Cabos or Mexico City."
  • Insurance (v3 wording, panel note open): "We don't accept insurance and can't bill it. Families pay directly, and it's a no on insurance, not a no on getting here. Most families use some mix of savings, an unsecured personal loan from a US lender (several of them lend up to $100,000 and pay the money to you, not to us), family help, and sometimes a fundraiser. I'll email a one-page list of the options with the invite. We're not lenders or advisors, so the list points you to the lenders and the government resources, and if you want someone to walk through it with you, tell me and I'll set that up."
  • Review: "The case review is free. It needs your most recent scans and pathology. The medical team reviews it; then you speak with the physician."
  • FDA (only when asked; panel note open): "FDA status depends on the specific drugs, devices and uses the physician would propose. Treatment is offered only in Mexico. The physician identifies each component's status after review."
  • Outcomes, safety, timeframes: reps never state them.

Never say

success rate, percentage, "most patients" · guarantee, cure, remission prediction · "no side effects," "minimally invasive" as a safety answer · any pathology statistic · "FDA approved / cleared / registered" · stop, delay or replace current treatment · interpreting scans or pathology · "you qualify / you're a candidate" · "we treat all types / all stages" · any price below $35,000 or a course figure the doctor hasn't set · "insurance might cover / reimburse" · "financing" or "payment plan" as a WCI product · "reverse mortgage," "home equity," "borrow against" as a suggestion · a specific APR or monthly payment · "the consultation is virtual" as a promise · stay length or duration before ratification · pulling hospital records ourselves · drug brand names · urgency or scarcity · "I understand how you feel" · any unscheduled dial to a lead · the price band or the procedure on a call the caller cannot finish.
